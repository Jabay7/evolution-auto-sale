Bookings and photos

False Advertising Law, Bus. & Prof. Code § 17500; rental rate disclosures, Civ. Code § 1939.19

This site shows no prices and takes no bookings or payments. Rates, fees, trip requirements and reservations are all handled on Turo, under Turo’s own terms.

Some images are digital 3D renderings based on vehicles in our fleet, not photographs. Details may differ slightly, and what’s available changes often, so check the listing on Turo before you book.

Privacy

California Online Privacy Protection Act, Bus. & Prof. Code §§ 22575–22579; California Consumer Privacy Act, Civ. Code § 1798.100 et seq.

We collect nothing. No forms, accounts, cookies, analytics, ads or trackers, and we never sell or share personal information. That also means we hold nothing about you to view, change or delete. Our web host keeps standard security logs.

Because nothing is tracked, “Do Not Track” browser settings change nothing here, and no other company can track you across websites through this one. If this policy changes, we’ll update this page. Effective October 2, 2026.

Accessibility

Americans with Disabilities Act, 42 U.S.C. § 12182; Unruh Civil Rights Act, Civ. Code §§ 51–52

We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 AA so everyone can use this site. If something is hard to use, tell us on Instagram @evo_a12 and we’ll fix it.
